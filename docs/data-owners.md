### Required attribution

When using KOVA3 in a publication, presentation, or derived resource, cite the
resource as described in [CITATION.md](../CITATION.md) and state the release
version used (for example, `KOVA3 v3.0.0`). Pinning the version matters:
allele frequencies change between releases as the cohort grows.

## Data ownership and provenance

KOVA3 is assembled and published by the **Choi Laboratory, Department of
Biomedical Sciences, Korea University College of Medicine**.

The underlying sequence data were generated by several independent Korean
genome projects, which retain ownership of their source data. The Choi
Laboratory publishes only the derived aggregate layer, under agreements with
each contributing institution.

| Contributing cohort | Source institution |
|---|---|
| National Integrated Bio-Big Data (국가통합바이오빅데이터) | KOBIC |
| Korea4K | KOGIC |
| Korea10K | KOGIC |
| Jeju Genome | Invites Genomics (인바이츠지노믹스) |

The Korea4K and Korea10K data were obtained from KOGIC; KOBIC acted as the
transfer route for part of that delivery but is not the originating institution
for those two cohorts.

### What ownership covers

The CC BY 4.0 grant applies to the **published aggregate layer only** — the
site-level allele counts, allele numbers, allele frequencies, homozygote
counts, call rates, filters, callability resources, and aggregate cohort
metadata described in this repository.

It does **not** extend to the underlying participant-level FASTQ, CRAM, gVCF,
or genotype data, which are not published here and remain under the control of
the contributing institutions.

## Privacy position

KOVA3 publishes aggregate site-level statistics only. No individual genotypes,
sequence reads, or participant identifiers are included, so the release does
not carry individual-level re-identification risk of the kind associated with
genotype-level data sharing.

## Disclaimer

KOVA3 is a research resource. Allele frequencies are estimates from a finite
cohort and carry sampling uncertainty, particularly for rare variants and in
regions of reduced callability. KOVA3 is not a clinical diagnostic device and
is not a substitute for clinical judgement. Users applying KOVA3 frequencies
in variant classification should consult the callability resources described in
[data-dictionary.md](data-dictionary.md) and account for allele number at each
site.
